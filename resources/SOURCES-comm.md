## Distillation method

Subtitles fetched with yt-dlp where available; three shorts (xM6PgakpLgU,
9i_LysEMddc, S0fTHpvCLOA) were rate-limited at the caption endpoint and
transcribed locally with faster-whisper (`small`, int8, VAD). Distilled in
four passes (one per talk, two for the shorts) and synthesized into the
skill. Auto-generated captions garble some names — "Zeltser" was
corrected from "Seltzer"; anything else uncertain was dropped rather
than guessed.
